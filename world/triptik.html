Home Lines of Fresno & Clovis

Home was Fresno and Clovis, in California’s Central Valley, from 2015 to 2025. Five lines leave from there, one for each commitment. The longest, line M, ends at 150+ community events, $240K+ raised and a $500K pledge for Fresno’s first Muslim women’s transitional home.

How to use this map

Tap a route bullet to run the highlighter along its line, the way a travel counselor marked a route. The strip maps below give every stop.

Days and years are service notes, the way a subway guide prints “rush hours only”.

Routes

KEY

  • Route bullet. One line per commitment.
  • Station. No dot, no stop: a dot only where there is a fact.
  • Terminal. Where a line ends.
  • Transfer station. Home: every line starts here.
  • Branch. The ends of a branch are not in any order.
  • Service note: when the line ran.
  • Highlighter. The route, marked by hand in fluorescent orange.
  • Pen. The counselor’s note.

Reference

Schematic. Not to scale. No streets are drawn and no place is located; lines show what connects, not where anything is.

Transfer. The Food Bank and Saturday School lines end in 2021, the year the M line opens.

Branches. The M line forks at VP & Executive Director into four ends: 150+ events, $240K+ raised, a $500K pledge, and the drives and mentoring groups. All four happened on that line; the map doesn’t rank them.
