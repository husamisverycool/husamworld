Home Lines of Fresno & Clovis

Home was Fresno and Clovis, in the Central Valley, from 2015 to 2025. Five lines leave from there, one for each commitment. Every stop is a fact.

How to use this map

Tap a route bullet to run the highlighter along its line. The strip maps below give every stop.

Days and years are service notes, the way a subway guide prints “rush hours only”.

Routes

KEY

  • Route bullet. One line per commitment.
  • Station. No dot, no stop: a dot only where there is a fact.
  • Terminal. Where a line ends.
  • Transfer station. Home: every line starts here.
  • Branch. The ends of a branch are not in any order.
  • Service note: when the line ran.
  • Highlighter. Marked by hand.
  • Pen. The counselor’s note.

SchematicNot to scale. No streets are drawn and no place is located; lines show what connects, not where anything is.
