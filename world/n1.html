Econometrics at three universities in ten months

His research started in economics, and it started fast: econometrics at Chapman University from July 2022, at the University of Nevada, Reno from that October, and at the University of Houston from January to March 2023, while the Reno work was still running.

At Reno he researched under Prof. Mark Pingle, on a paper that asks its question in the title.

One of 12 fellows, from more than 90 applicants

Then he moved from markets to policy: Public Health Policy Analyst at the Institute for Youth in Policy in 2023, and Researcher at The World in Us from 2023 into 2024.

In January 2024 Ballotpedia named him a Research Fellow, one of 12 chosen from more than 90 applicants, through March. How selective is that? Guess before you look.

Three roles at once at the Institute of Politics

In the summer of 2025 the Institute of Politics named him a Fellow in its Policy Program in Economics, researching the impact of international students on Harvard and U.S. universities.

That September the Institute of Politics at Harvard Kennedy School put him in three roles at once, through May 2026. He was an analyst on the Harvard Youth Poll, America’s largest poll of 18–29-year-olds. He was associate to Ambassador Katherine Tai, the 19th U.S. Trade Representative. And he consulted for the Red Cross and the United Nations.

At the same time he was a Senior Associate at the Harvard Global Research and Consulting Group, through February 2026, consulting for UNICEF and the Urban Institute, with work for the WHO and the UN as well.

So what does the record add up to?

Read back through it and one pattern holds, from an economics department in Reno to a national youth poll: he learns how a place asks its questions first, then does the asking himself.

Methods

What counts as a post. Every research, polling, analysis or consulting role in his record counts once, in the year it began. Eleven in all.

A judgment call. The Institute of Politics is counted as four posts (the summer fellowship, the poll, the associate role, the consulting) because they were four different jobs. Counted as one, 2025 adds two posts instead of five, and the total is eight.

What isn’t here. No results from the Harvard Youth Poll appear on this page, and no findings from any paper. They aren’t part of his record, so nothing was charted from them.
