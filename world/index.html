One road, nine doors

The road runs from Fresno to Cambridge and wraps once around a very small planet. Every landmark on it opens onto a page.

    Driving: WASD or the arrows, Shift to boost, Space to brake (twice to hop), H for the horn, R to get back on the road, Enter to go in, M for this map. On a phone, the stick and the four buttons.

    The light follows your own clock. A homage to Bruno Simon’s folio and Abeto’s Messenger; every shape, sound and letter here is drawn in code.
