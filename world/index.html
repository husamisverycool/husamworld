    Every fact on one plain page: no driving, and it prints as a résumé.

    One road, nine doors

    The road runs from Fresno to Cambridge and wraps once around a very small planet. Every landmark on it opens onto a page.

      Driving: WASD or the arrows, Shift to boost, Space to brake (twice to hop), H for the horn, R to get back on the road, Enter to go in, M for this map, P for the plain page. On a phone, put a thumb down on the left half and push; tap the car to honk. Click or tap any landmark’s sign and the car drives itself there.

      The light follows your own clock. A homage to Bruno Simon’s folio and Abeto’s Messenger; every shape, sound and letter here is drawn in code.
