Route:

Every place the work has reached, in date order. The lines start in Fresno; Harvard's trips branch from Cambridge. The globe follows as you scroll.

  1. 2015–2025 · home

    The Central Valley. Volunteered at the Central California Food Bank most Sundays, 2015–2021, and at Masjid Fresno Saturday School most Saturdays, 2018–2021.

    Vice President & Executive Director of the Muslim Community Association of Fresno: 150+ community events, $240K+ raised for Syrian and Afghan refugees and Pakistan flood victims, and a $500K pledge secured for Fresno's first Muslim women's transitional home.

  2. May 2021–May 2023 · CAIR

    Lead community advocate for AB 1766, California IDs for All. Also advocated for AB 1947 (Freedom from Hate Crimes), SB 1161 (Transit Safety), AB 2549 (Ending Street Harassment) and SB 1038 (limits on biometric surveillance by law enforcement).

    Five California bills, worked end to end.

  3. Jul–Oct 2022 · Chapman University

    Econometrics and economics research with Chapman University. The first of three universities he researched with in 2022–2023.

  4. Jul 2022–Apr 2024, summer 2024

    White House in Arabic: Senior English Writer. Edited and translated press briefings, op-eds and documentary scripts between Arabic and English under Marah Bukai, Washington Bureau Chief of Al-Arab Media Organization. 350K+ readers and viewers.

    Summer 2024: the Telluride Association Summer Seminar in Washington DC–Baltimore, 1 of 72 selected from 2,400+ applicants. Elected house chair.

  5. Oct 2022–Apr 2023 · University of Nevada, Reno

    Econometrics research under Prof. Mark Pingle, on the paper “Can Private Enterprise do for Health Care What it Has Done for Food?”

  6. Jan–Mar 2023 · University of Houston

    Econometrics research with the University of Houston.

  7. Fall 2025– · Harvard College

    Applied Math in Government and Economics.

    Chair (Senior Staff) of Harvard Model Congress since January 2026, on staff since September 2025. He is directing the Boston conference, North America's largest congressional simulation: 1,500+ delegates from 100+ schools.

    The roles that stay in Cambridge are at the gates, below.

  8. Harvard Hillel Travel Program

    Harvard Hillel Travel Program Fellow: a research tour of Lithuania, Poland and Hungary. One of Harvard's Service, Ethics, and Leadership Fellowships.

  9. Catalyst

    Catalyst Sustainability Scholar: a service-learning trip to Puerto Rico.

  10. Harvard Model Congress Europe

    On staff for HMC Europe: 500+ delegates from 20+ countries. The globe shades the continent rather than pinning a city.

  11. Since Oct 2025 · The Harvard Crimson

    As a Global Programs Associate for the nation's oldest daily college newspaper (est. 1873), he traveled to South Korea to mentor students and lead two weeks of workshops on writing, leadership and journalism. He also moderated and judged international semifinal and final rounds of the Harvard Crimson Global Quiz Bowl.

    Since December 2025: Project Team Lead for TPR/AlgoEd on the Crimson Business Board, leading international writing and leadership camps with The Princeton Review and AlgoEd.

  12. Summer 2026

    Co-founder & CEO of a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash's early team. He spent summer 2026 building it full-time in San Francisco.

    We'd tell you more, but then it wouldn't be stealth.
