# Who owns what: every fact and every device lives in exactly ONE place

Husam's rule: **nothing written or mentioned in one section may be written or mentioned in another.** Every fact in `versions/CONTENT.md` has exactly one owner below. A page may show ONLY its own facts. It may *point* to another district by name ("that's in the Capitol"), and that is the only cross-reference allowed. No restating, summarizing, or teasing another page's facts, numbers, organizations or places.

The same goes for design devices: each signature device belongs to one page. Nobody else uses it, even in a small way.
