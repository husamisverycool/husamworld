# The Garage, round 2: decision

Three working prototypes, screenshots in `world/_shots/os-{a,b,c}-{1440,390}-*.png`.

- **a.html, 95 elevated.** Henry Heffernan + daedalOS, as round 1, plus two new ideas tested in 2D: the garage-myth opening shot (the driveway at night, the roll-up door rising, a bronze plaque by the door, after the HP and Apple garages) and Henry's *My Showcase* window as the home (a left nav of big underlined serif links, pages per build) in place of the About window.
- **b.html, classic Mac.** system.css / platinum.css values: 20 px white menu bar, pinstriped title bars with close and zoom boxes, 2px 2px hard shadows, a checkerboard scrollbar, a blue-grey dither desktop, disk / locked folder / Trash on the right. Boot: a compact-Mac outline with a garage door where the Happy Mac's face goes, "Welcome to the Garage.", the grants marching along the bottom as extensions. Electric Deals as an "About This Store" memory-bar window.
- **c.html, poolsuite.** One flat pastel, one centred hero window (a "player" whose video is a dithered garage door rolling up and down and whose "tracks" are the builds, the stealth startup playing first), a bottom dock of square tiles, top tabs "GARAGE OS · EST. 2020" and "SAN FRANCISCO · SUMMER 2026" (poolsuite's date tab, without its clock).

## Decision: (a), the current page, honestly wins, and gets elevated

The classic Mac direction is well-made but quieter and less Husam-specific; poolsuite is a brand mood that doesn't fit the only page holding his company. The 95 garage wins on every axis that matters, and the two new ideas make it better:

1. **The opening shot is the garage myth.** The camera starts on the driveway at night, the door closed, a warm line of light under it, a bronze plaque on the wall. The four door sections roll up along their tracks (the HP/Apple image everyone knows), light spills onto the concrete, and the camera walks in to Henry's idle view of the bench. START sits at the CRT exactly as before. This replaces nothing Henry-faithful; it adds the one image garage-startup mythology is built on.
2. **The plaque** ("THE GARAGE · things built here": Electric Deals 2020, bayloop 2023, a redacted line for 2026) sits by the door outside and replaces the wall calendar inside (a calendar is the Plaza's).
3. **The home window is Henry's *My Showcase*,** rebuilt for the builds: a left nav (HOME, NOW, ELECTRIC DEALS, BAYLOOP, JETSON, BACK OFFICE, GRANTS) in bold underlined serif and pages in Millennium-style body type, opening each program from its page. No photo, no name hero, no contact, no Harvard, no Fresno-as-biography.
4. **Removed** (ownership): the About window and photo, LinkedIn and `[email]`, "Applied Math…Harvard College", "Languages: EN, AR", "Fresno, CA" as his location (the BIOS's "Detecting operator", the neofetch "Host"), "Current location: Cambridge", the Credits program, the live HUD clock, the taskbar clock and its calendar, the wall calendar, the Mines timer, the BIOS/loader date, and the "100+ sales" and "home goods in bulk / pandemic demand" lines, which are not in CONTENT.md.
5. **Replaced with Garage-owned things:** the tray shows "Uptime: since 03/2020" (the OS has been running since Electric Deals); the Mines right-hand LED counts flags placed, not seconds; the HUD types "The Garage" and "things built here".
