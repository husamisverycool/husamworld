    IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

    Mr. Sokar introduced the following bill; which was referred to the reader, for signature

    A BILL

    To record five California bills, one House office, and the organizing that carried them, and for other purposes.

    Legislative Counsel’s Digest

    Existing record shows a lead community advocate in Sacramento for two years, working five California bills; a congressional intern in a district office; an executive director of a global civil-rights nonprofit; and one of five executives of a national nonprofit of student organizers.

    This bill would put that record into law, one section per role, and would take effect when the reader signs it.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

    SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the “Worked End to End Act”.

    TITLE I—THE CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE

    SEC. 101. LEAD ADVOCATE.

    SacramentoMay 2021–May 2023. Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR). Two years in the building.

    Mr. Sokar served as Lead Advocate for CAIR in Sacramento, California, from May 2021 to May 2023.

    SEC. 102. THE MEASURES.

    Two housesAssembly: Asm. Jim Patterson’s office, through Legislative Director Ian Coolbear. Senate: Sen. Shannon Grove’s office.

    (a) Lead Measure.—He was the lead community advocate for AB 1766 (California IDs for All), working with the office of Assemblymember Jim Patterson, through Legislative Director Ian Coolbear, and with the office of Senator Shannon Grove.

    (b) Further Measures.—He also advocated for⁠—

    1. (1) AB 1947, Freedom from Hate Crimes;
    2. (2) SB 1161, Transit Safety;
    3. (3) AB 2549, Ending Street Harassment; and
    4. (4) SB 1038, limits on biometric surveillance by law enforcement.

    (c) Count.—Five California bills, worked end to end.

    As amendedApril–December 2022. Text struck through is the earlier title; italic is the title as amended.
