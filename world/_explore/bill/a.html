Mr. Sokar introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on the Judiciary, and in addition to the Committee on House Administration

A BILL

To record five California bills, one congressional office, and the organizing that carried them, and for other purposes.

Legislative Counsel’s Digest

Existing record: a lead community advocate in Sacramento for two years; a congressional intern in Clovis; an executive director of a global Muslim civil-rights nonprofit; one of five executives of a 5,000-organizer national nonprofit. This bill would set that record in type.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Worked End to End Act”.

TITLE I—THE CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE

SEC. 101. CALIFORNIA LEGISLATION.

May 2021–May 2023. Council on American-Islamic Relations (CAIR), Sacramento. Lead Advocate.

(a) In General.—As Lead Advocate for CAIR in Sacramento, Mr. Sokar was the lead community advocate for AB 1766 (California IDs for All), working with the office of Assemblymember Jim Patterson, through Legislative Director Ian Coolbear, and with the office of Senator Shannon Grove.

(b) Further Measures.—He also advocated for AB 1947, Freedom from Hate Crimes; SB 1161, Transit Safety; AB 2549, Ending Street Harassment; and SB 1038, limits on biometric surveillance by law enforcement.

(c) Count.—Five California bills, worked end to end.

TITLE II—THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

SEC. 201. DISTRICT OFFICE.

June–December 2022. U.S. House of Representatives, Clovis, California.

Mr. Sokar served as a Congressional Intern in the Office of Kevin McCarthy, in Clovis, California.

TITLE III—ORGANIZING

SEC. 301. LEGISLATIVELIFT.

Amendment. volunteer Outreach Director Executive Director, April–December 2022.

Mr. Sokar served as a volunteer Outreach Director Executive Director of LegislativeLift, a global Muslim civil-rights nonprofit of more than 11,000 members and 80 volunteers, with events in Egypt, India, and the United States, running scholar programs and legislative literacy.

SEC. 302. DIVERSIFY OUR NARRATIVE.

As Executive Director of Operations, one of five executives, he helped lead a national 501(c)(3) of more than 5,000 student organizers and 800 district chapters pushing curriculum equity reform, featured in TIME, Bloomberg, Vox, ABC Nightline, and The Washington Post.
