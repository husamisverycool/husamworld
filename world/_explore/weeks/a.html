Note: This page doesn't say what any of it was. It only counts how many things were going on at once, and says which district of this world each one lives in. Tap a week.

The busiest stretch: 7 things at once, every week from October 1 to December 31, 2025.

Each row is a year. Each box is a week. The darker the box, the more things were going on at once. The dots in the corners are weekends.

2015–2019. For five years the weekdays are empty. The only marks are the weekend dots: most Sundays from 2015, and from 2018 most Saturdays too.

2020. The first weekday boxes fill in: one thing at a time, all in the Garage.

2022. The boxes go dark. By October, 7 things at once, for 4 weeks.

2024. The widest week: things in 5 different districts at once.

2025. Back to 7, and this time it held for 13 weeks straight.

That's every week. Tap any box to see which districts it touched.
