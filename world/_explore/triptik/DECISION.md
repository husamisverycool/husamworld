# The Map Room, round 2: decision

Three working prototypes, each built on a different reference. Screenshots: `world/_shots/triptik-r2-{a,b,c}-{1440,390}.png` (full page).

- **A. TripTik city strips** (`a.html`): five spiral-bound strips side by side, one per commitment, a red road from HOME to a green destination sign, highlighter wipe on view, pen-blue notes, a log box under each strip.
- **B. Vignelli "lines"** (`b.html`): a 45/90 service diagram on beige land with a tan river, one colored line and route bullet per commitment, a years axis, an interchange box at 2021, and a pocket-guide "service breakdown" underneath.
- **C. Grape-lug label + bulletin board** (`c.html`): a navy grape-lug crate label masthead with arched extruded caps, sun, Sierra and vineyard rows; then a cork board of copier-paper flyers with pushpins, a marker thermometer and tear-off tabs.

## What I learned from building them
1. **An honest schematic TripTik of home is empty.** Without inventing streets, a strip from HOME to the Food Bank is a squiggle. The strip format only earns its keep when the strip carries *information*: stations, service notes, numbers. That is exactly what a subway car's **strip map** does (the one-line diagram over the doors), and strip maps are my device.
2. **"Weekdays as stations" needs facts I don't have.** CONTENT gives days only for the Food Bank (most Sundays) and the Saturday School (most Saturdays). A weekday station for the MCA, the MSA or tutoring would be invented, and a row of weekday stations is the Plaza's week grid by another name. Vignelli's own pocket guide solves it: days are **service notes** on a route ("Sundays only"), not stations.
3. **A years axis turns lines into lanes**, which is the Plaza's picture. So the diagram should not be laid out on time at all. It should be a **network with HOME as the transfer station**: every line leaves from Fresno & Clovis, runs its own stops, and ends at its terminal. Years become station and service labels, which is how the 1972 guide printed hours.
4. **Geography must stay out.** Even putting the MSA in a "Clovis" zone is a location claim I can't make. The diagram is pure topology, and the key says so.
5. **The crate label is the page's voice.** Nothing else on the site speaks the Central Valley's own graphic language; it makes the page unmistakably from Fresno. It works as the masthead, not the whole page.
6. **The bulletin board is best in one dose.** One MCA notice with real tear-off tabs is delightful; a whole wall of flyers reads as cards.

## Decision: combine B's system, C's voice, and A's hand
**"Home Lines."** A Vignelli-disciplined transit system of home, printed like a pocket guide from the Central Valley.

- **Masthead: a grape-lug crate label** (C), redrawn for phones as a near-square citrus-box label instead of shrinking the strip. Arched extruded "FRESNO · CLOVIS", script "Home Lines" brand, sun over the Sierra and vineyard rows, bottom band "Grown in the Central Valley · Fresno & Clovis, California".
- **System map: hub and spokes** (B, fixed). HOME is the interchange: five black dots joined by a bar. Five lines run out at 0/45/90 degrees with route bullets. Stations only where a fact exists ("no dot, no stop"). The MCA line branches at the VP & Executive Director station into four terminals (150+ events; $240K+; the $500K pledge; drives and mentoring), so no order among them is implied. Days and years are service notes. Redrawn in portrait for phones, never scaled.
- **Strip maps: one per line** (A's device in its subway form). Each line gets an in-car strip map with stations, service notes and the counselor's pen-blue notes with hand-drawn arrows (Maggie Appleton). Horizontal on desktop; on phones they turn and run top to bottom like TripTik strips.
- **The highlighter** (mine): choosing a route bullet, a line or a strip runs a fluorescent highlighter along that line on the system map and on its strip; the others dim. It is the only motion, and reduced motion gets the mark without the wipe.
- **KEY** panel in Sanborn form (notched "KEY" banner, double-rule frame) explaining bullets, stations, the interchange, service notes, the highlighter and the pen, and stating that the map is schematic: no streets, nothing located.
- **One bulletin-board notice** at the MCA terminal with tear-off tabs you can actually tear (the text is also printed on the notice, so nothing is lost).

Dropped: the car, all non-home places, the photo, the cover form "prepared for", spiral binding on every strip (kept once on the pocket guide), the years axis, weekday stations.

No intro: the masthead is the opening, and it is complete at rest.
