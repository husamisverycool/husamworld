Here's the big one. Everything below is a real site I know of or that surfaced in current roundups; personal sites change often, so a few may have been redesigned since I last saw them (which is half the fun of visiting).
Part 1 — Personal websites worth studying
The interactive / "holy crap" tier

* bruno-simon.com — drive a 3D car around his résumé. The most-copied personal site of the decade.
* dennissnellenberg.com — loading transition greets you in multiple languages, then a minimal layout with award-winning smooth interactions. [Wedoflow](https://www.wedoflow.com/post/best-inspirational-portfolio-websites)
* yasio.dev — Jan Baszczok's site: a code-editor look filled with smooth 3D interactions, dark, one-page. [Wedoflow](https://www.wedoflow.com/post/best-inspirational-portfolio-websites)
* rauno.me — Rauno Freiberg's playground of micro-interactions; possibly the best "craft" site on the web.
* paco.me — Paco Coursey, quiet, dark, perfect type, tiny details everywhere.
* emilkowal.ski — Emil Kowalski, animation-focused, each element feels physical.
* joshwcomeau.com — interactive, visually rich blog posts packed with real teaching; sound effects, a dark-mode toggle that's a genuine delight. [Curious](https://curious.page/blog/best-personal-website-examples-developers)
* brittanychiang.com — the gold standard for developer sites for years: clean, immediately tells you who she is and where she's worked. [Curious](https://curious.page/blog/best-personal-website-examples-developers)
* lynnandtonic.com — Lynn Fisher redesigns yearly; resize the browser window and watch what happens.
* jhey.dev — Jhey Tompkins, CSS/GSAP wizardry, a bear that follows your cursor.
* cassie.codes — Cassie Evans, SVG-animated site with a scrolling character.
* sarahdrasner.com — SVG animation pioneer's home.
* robbowen.digital — Rob Bowen, generative visuals and 3D.
* henry.codes / henryheffernan.com — Henry Heffernan's site is a 3D-rendered PC you actually boot into; there's a working desktop OS inside.
* dustinbrett.com — a full working web desktop as a personal site.
* ryo.lu — Ryo Lu (Notion design), ambient and strange.
* benjamin.gg / benjaminlittlefield — sort of a game-console UI.
* chen.gg, ronny.fyi, davidhaz.com — Awwwards-circuit developers with heavy WebGL.
* keitakei.com and mostafa-taha types on the awwwards portfolio index; the index itself is worth trawling (awwwards.com/websites/portfolio).

The typography-and-restraint tier (mostly text, all taste)

* frankchimero.com — long-form essays, immaculate type.
* rsms.me — Rasmus Andersson (Inter typeface); nerdy and beautiful.
* craigmod.com — writer/walker; huge margins, photography, newsletters.
* jasonsantamaria.com — the classic art-directed blog.
* markboulton.co.uk, robinrendle.com, chriscoyier.net — front-end elder statesmen with hand-built sites.
* maggieappleton.com — the "digital garden" archetype: notes with growth stages, illustrations, wiki links.
* gwern.net — dense, sidenote-laden, hover-popup link previews; the most extreme reader-focused site.
* sive.rs — Derek Sivers, brutally simple, loads instantly.
* kottke.org — 25+ years of one person's blog.
* paulstamatiou.com — photography + long gear posts.
* leerob.io — Vercel's Lee Robinson, the Next.js "minimal dev site" template that thousands cloned.
* rafa.design, mds.is (Matt D. Smith), andy.works, benji.org, alexanderobenauer.com.
* world.hey.com/dhh and world.hey.com/jason — Basecamp founders; pure text.
* danluu.com and jvns.ca — deliberately unstyled/handmade; proof content wins.
* stephango.com — Steph Ango (Obsidian CEO); Flexoki color scheme, calm.
* brianlovin.com, hamel.dev, jxnl.co, simonwillison.net — dev/AI writers with personality.

Playful / weird / delightful

* nicolasglinoer or the "Website of the Year" crowd on godly.website (search "portfolio" tag).

* hakim.se — Hakim El Hattab (reveal.js), experiments gallery.

* tholman.com — Tim Holman, experiments (obnoxious.css, elevator.js).

Product / design leaders

Old-school / brutalist / tiny web
