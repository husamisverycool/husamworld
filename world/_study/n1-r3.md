## Measured Pudding specifics (from the scrape)
- Body: Tiempos Text, 20px, colour **#4E4E4E**, ~1.4–1.55 line height, border-radius 0 everywhere.
- Headlines: Tiempos Headline **bold**, ~48px, same #4E4E4E, sentence case, often a **finding or a
  question** ("There are 7.0% excess birthday deaths in Massachusetts.", "So what have we learned?",
  "But…").
- Header: wordmark centred on top, byline "By Russell Samora" small at top right. No kicker, no
  labelled byline grid.
- Charts: **no frame**, title centred over the plot, **series named by coloured words** in the title
  ("births and deaths per month") instead of a legend, **numbers written straight onto the chart in
  the series colour** ("183 birthday deaths", "average: 156", "12 days after"), labels with a white
  halo where they cross marks.
- Bold: one key phrase per paragraph, not every noun.
- Links in prose: underlined, text colour.
- Opener (democracy): title owns the first screen, a lone centred "↓" near the bottom.
- End: "Data and methods" as a small bold heading in the body serif, then plain paragraphs and an
  asterisk note.
- The only all-caps on those pages is the site footer's mono "ABOUT US / FOLLOW US", not in stories.

## The detail nobody asked for
The optical reader now keys off the timing marks the way a real OMR head does: as the red scan line
passes each row, that row's black timing mark flashes red for 90ms, then the printer mark appears.
Blank and multi-answer rows print a correct key ("— all" instead of a clipped "— ABCDE").
