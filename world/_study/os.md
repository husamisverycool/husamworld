# Study: henryheffernan.com + dustinbrett.com (daedalOS) → `world/os.html`

Sources read: live scrapes (henryheffernan.com, os.henryheffernan.com, dustinbrett.com, with screenshots) and the source of
github.com/henryjeff/portfolio-website (outer 3D room), github.com/henryjeff/portfolio-inner-site (inner OS),
github.com/DustinBrett/daedalOS.

## Henry Heffernan, outer site (the room)
- **Order of events:** black BIOS-style loading screen → centered START popup → overlay fades (opacity 0 + scale 1.1, 200 ms ease-in-out) → camera flies from a far "loading" keyframe to an idle orbit (2500 ms, Exponential.Out) → hover the monitor → camera pushes into the screen (2000 ms, `BezierEasing(0.13, 0.99, 0, 1)`) → leave the monitor → camera returns to a "desk" keyframe (1000 ms, Quintic.InOut).
- **Loading/BIOS screen (`LoadingScreen.tsx`):** black, `monospace` 16 px, `letter-spacing: .8px`, white text, bold highlights in `#00ff00`. Header: two-line green bold vendor block ("Heffernan, / Henry Inc.") with, 64 px to its right, "Released: 01/13/2000" and "HHBIOS (C)2000 Heffernan Henry Inc.,". Body: "HSP S13 2000-2022 Special UC131S", "HSP Showcase(tm) XX 113", "Checking RAM : 14000 OK", "LOADING RESOURCES (n/N)" with animated dots (`content: '' → '.' → '..' → '...'`, 1 s steps), then a list of "Loaded <name><padding> ... NN%" lines, then "All Content Loaded, launching **'Henry Heffernan Portfolio Showcase'** V1.0". Footer: "Press **DEL** to enter SETUP , **ESC** to skip memory test" and today's date as MM/DD/YYYY. Padding 48 px (16 px on phones), footer bottom 64 px.
- **Blinking cursor:** a white bar `0.8em × 0.15em` (an underscore), `blink 0.65s step-end infinite`.
- **START popup:** black box, `border: 7px solid #fff`, padding 24, max-width 500. "Henry Heffernan Portfolio Showcase 2022" / "Click start to begin" + cursor / a START button with `border: 4px solid #fff; border-width: 4px 3px` whose label inverts (white bg, black text) on hover. Phones get a yellow "WARNING: This experience is best viewed on a desktop" line.
- **Info overlay after start (`InfoOverlay.tsx`):** top-left stack of black boxes (padding 4/16, 4 px apart), white monospace text *typed* one character at a time (50–100 ms random per char): name → title → live clock (`toLocaleTimeString`, ticking every second) → then two small square toggles fade in 250 ms apart (mute, free-cam). 64 px from the edges (24 px on mobile).
- **Monitor screen:** the real inner site is an `<iframe>` in a `CSS3DObject` (1280×1024, 32 px padding, container bg `#1d2e2f`), occluded by a WebGL plane with `NoBlending`. On top, stacked glass layers at small depth offsets: fingerprint **smudges** (additive, opacity 0.12), an **inner shadow** vignette (normal, 1.0), two **video noise/flicker** layers (additive 0.5 and 0.1), side planes `#48493f` closing the gap, and a **perspective dimmer** plane whose opacity rises with viewing angle and distance (`DIM_FACTOR 0.7`). The iframe itself has a `.jitter` animation: sub-pixel translate keyframes (≤0.25 px), 0.3 s infinite.
- **Room:** an isolated desk in a soft gray void; beige CRT on a desktop case with a floppy slot and power button, keyboard, mouse, paper trays, binders, mug, plant, brown office chair. All baked lighting. The bezel carries a small wordmark with his name ("Heffernan henry inc.").
- **Camera:** idle keyframe swings slowly on a sine (`x = sin((t+19000)*0.00008) * origin.x`); desk keyframe follows the mouse with lerps of 0.05 (focus) and 0.025 (position).
- **Audio:** office ambience loop through a low-pass filter whose cutoff rises with camera distance; startup sound on start; mouse-down/up clicks and keyboard key sounds when interacting inside the computer; auto-typed text triggers soft key ticks.

## Henry Heffernan, inner OS (`portfolio-inner-site`)
- **Palette (`colors.ts`):** turquoise desktop `#3e9697`, light gray `#c3c6ca`, dark gray `#86898d`, title-bar blue `#0000a3`, highlight `#0000aa`, white, black. Shutdown screen bg `#1d2e2f`.
- **Fonts:** MS Sans Serif (bitmap) for chrome 12–14 px; Millennium / Millennium Bold (pixel serif) for body 18 px and h2–h4; a heavy soft serif (gastromond) for the 64–72 px name; lores-15-bold for window titles; Terminal for the Start menu banner.
- **Window (`Window.tsx`):** outer 1 px border (TL `#c3c6ca`, BR black), inner 1 px (TL white, BR `#86898d`), 2 px padding. Title bar 20 px `#0000a3`, 16 px icon, white bold title; three buttons 16×14 (min, max, then 2 px gap, close) with the same two-tone bevel, hover darkens to `#86898d`. Content well: 8 px above/below, two inset borders (TL dark gray/black, BR white/light gray), white fill. Bottom status bar 20 px: an inset text segment (flex 5/7, 12 px "© Copyright 2022 Henry Heffernan"), two 16 px inset spacers, an inset segment with the diagonal resize grip. Inactive windows: title bar `#86898d`, title `#c3c6ca`, icon at 50 %.
- **Dragging is outline-only:** while dragging/resizing, a 6 px checkerboard frame (black 4 px checker on white) follows the pointer with `mix-blend-mode: difference`; the window jumps there on mouse-up. Resize minimum 520×220.
- **Desktop shortcuts:** 56 px column at left 6/top 16, every 104 px, scaled ×1.5. 32 px icon, 8 px white MS Sans Serif label. Single click selects (icon gets a blue checkerboard mask in the icon's own shape; label gets `#0000a3` bg + dotted white border), double-click (300 ms) opens. The last-selected label keeps a dotted border.
- **Taskbar (`Toolbar.tsx`):** 32 px, `#c3c6ca`, 1 px white top highlight. Start button: raised bevel, 18 px logo + "Start". Task buttons: flex 1, max 300 px, 4 px apart, raised; the active one is pressed with a 4 px white checkerboard. Tray: inset, 86×24, speaker icon + time "9:41 PM" (12 px), updated every 5 s. Clicking the active task minimizes it.
- **Start menu:** 256 px wide, sits on the taskbar at left 4. Left vertical banner in dark gray with "HeffernanOS" set in Terminal 32 px, light gray, rotated to read bottom-to-top. Items 24 px + 12 px padding, 32 px icon, 14 px text; hover `#0000aa` with white text. Contents: just a separator and "Sh<u>u</u>t down...".
- **Shutdown sequence:** 600 ms after choosing it, the screen goes `#1d2e2f`, a blinking cursor waits 2 s, then a typed log (20 ms/char, `>ms<` pause tokens, `|…|` dumps whole lines) that fails and reboots; later attempts escalate the joke.
- **Boot:** opens "My Showcase" by default. Apps: Showcase (a site in a window with a 300 px vertical nav: HOME, ABOUT, EXPERIENCE, PROJECTS, CONTACT in bold underlined serif), The Oregon Trail, Doom, Scrabble (js-dos), Henordle (Wordle clone), Credits (slides every 5 s, click to advance, sections "Engineering & Design", "Sound Design", "Special Thanks", "Inspiration").

## Dustin Brett, daedalOS
- A Windows-10-like desktop in the browser, dark (`#000`) with an animated **Milky Way** wallpaper (also Waves, Hexells, Matrix; set via right-click → Properties / "Set as wallpaper").
- Desktop icons: white labels with a stacked black text-shadow; focus/hover tints `hsla(207, …)`; drag on empty desktop draws a **selection rectangle** (`hsla(207,100%,45%,30%)` fill, 1 px border); icons can be dragged to arrange; arrows/Enter/F2/Delete keyboard shortcuts; double-click 500 ms; long-press 500 ms on touch.
- **Windows** animate: open from `scale .95, opacity 0` to 1 (250 ms); close back to .95/0; **minimize flies to the taskbar entry** (translate to its center, `scale .7`, opacity 0); maximize fills the viewport minus the 30 px taskbar.
- **Taskbar:** 30 px, `hsla(0,0%,10%,70%)` with blur; Start (π glyph), Search; entries with a focused-window indicator and hover **Peek** previews; **clock with seconds** ("9:42:05 PM"), date tooltip ("September 25, 2026 / Friday"), click opens a calendar.
- **Context menus** everywhere (Sort by, Refresh, New Folder / Text Document, Properties…), a **Run** dialog, a real **Terminal** with `dir/ls, cd, type/cat, cls/clear, help, ver, whoami, date, time, uptime, neofetch/systeminfo (ASCII logo + OS/Host/Kernel/Uptime/Resolution/Theme lines + 16 color swatches), color, echo, history, shutdown, sheep`.
- File Explorer with address bar, back/forward/up, Details and Thumbnail views, status bar item counts.

## What I take into the Garage (and what I change)
- Henry's arc, reordered to the brief: **dim garage first** (three.js, procedural geometry and canvas textures, no models) → Henry's START popup → CRT power-on → camera push into the glass → BIOS POST in his exact layout (vendor block, Released line, typed lines, DEL/ESC footer, date) with Husam's lines → a 95-style cloud splash with a moving bar → HusamOS 95 desktop, synthesized chime (opt-in).
- Henry's glass: scanlines, inner-shadow vignette, smudge and flicker as layers over the DOM; sub-pixel jitter on BIOS text only. The CRT in the garage shows a Starfield screensaver (a period screensaver, and a nod to Dustin's galaxy wallpaper) until START.
- Henry's inner-OS chrome, measured values and colors verbatim; outline dragging and resizing with the difference-blended checkerboard; Start menu with a vertical banner; clock in the tray; shutdown log in `#1d2e2f` then the orange "It's now safe to turn off your computer" screen.
- Dustin's system layer: open/close/minimize-to-taskbar transitions (250 ms), selection rectangle, draggable icons, right-click desktop menu → Display Properties with animated wallpapers (Starfield, Clouds), Run dialog, a working command prompt (dir/type/cls/help/ver/whoami/date/time/neofetch/start/shutdown), clock tooltip + calendar.
- Changes for Husam: no Microsoft names or logos (HusamOS 95, a garage-door mark in place of the flag, "This Computer" as Henry named it), the Start button moved right so the bottom-left 72 px stays free, phones get full-screen sheets and single-tap icons, and everything is also a plain page (`#page`) for no-JS and reduced-motion visitors.
- Fonts (Google): VT323 (BIOS, prompt, overlay), Pixelify Sans (chrome, ≈ MS Sans Serif), Tinos (Times New Roman metrics ≈ Millennium body), Caprasimo (Cooper-style heavy serif ≈ gastromond for the name), Courier Prime (text files).
