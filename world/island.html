    One road, nine doors

    The road runs from Fresno to Cambridge, once round a small island. Every landmark on it opens onto a page. Press a pin on the map to go straight there.

      Driving: WASD or the arrows, Shift to boost, B to brake, Space to jump, H for the horn, R to get back on the road, Enter to go in, M for this map, P for the plain page. On a phone, touch the ground and the car drives toward your finger; tap the car to jump. Click or tap any landmark’s sign and the car drives itself there.

      The light follows your own clock. A homage to Bruno Simon’s folio (its driving, camera and touch ring are adapted from his MIT-licensed code) and Abeto’s Messenger; every shape, sound and letter here is drawn in code.
