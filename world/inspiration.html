What this site was built from

Every place in husam.world is a close study of something real. These are the sources, place by place, and what each one gave.

The world index

  • Bruno Simon, folio-2025: a world you drive around instead of a page you scroll.
  • Bruno Simon, folio-2019: the little car, and landmarks you drive up to as doors.
  • Messenger by Abeto: the little planet on the loading screen and the title, and people who talk as you pass.

Capitol bill

  • congress.gov and the GPO bill print: the tracker, the actions table and the typeset bill.
  • gwern.net: notes that sit in the margin beside the line they explain.

Chamber speech

  • Hatch Show Print: wood-type fight posters, every line fitted to the measure.
  • NCAA scoreboards: the scores bar, the bracket and the scorebug.
  • dennissnellenberg.com: the round button whose fill slides out on leave.

Bridge translation

  • Library of Arabic Literature (NYU Press): facing Arabic and English pages and plain chapter openings.
  • Loeb Classical Library: the small, serious parallel-text book.
  • Amiri: the Arabic type, a revival of the Bulaq Press naskh.

Polling Station n1

  • The Pudding: one person running a small study in front of you.
  • Pew toplines: the routing line, the question ID and the plain response table.
  • Scantron forms: the bubbles and the form furniture.

Map Room triptik

  • AAA TripTik: strip maps in a spiral booklet, with an orange highlighter along the route.
  • Vignelli's 1972 subway map: straight lines, clean angles and a colour for each line.
  • Sanborn maps: the notched KEY banner.

Garage os

  • henryheffernan.com: a computer you boot into, with a Start popup and a showcase.
  • dustinbrett.com (daedalOS): windows that open and minimize, a Run box and a terminal.

Pier depths

  • neal.fun/deep-sea: one long scroll down, with a counter that keeps running as you go.

Plaza weeks

  • Wait But Why, “Your Life in Weeks”: a life drawn as a grid of small boxes.
  • Feltron annual reports: a year of one person's own numbers, set as a report.
  • GitHub's contribution graph: week columns, day rows and five shades of green.

Airport everywhere

  • play.ertdfgcvb.xyz (Andreas Gysin): a globe drawn in characters.
  • Solari split-flap boards: departures that flip letter by letter.
  • Otl Aicher's pictograms: the signs, drawn on a 45 and 90 degree grid.

The short version facts

  • danluu.com: no webfonts, nothing required to load, the words first.
  • Steph Ango's Flexoki: the paper and ink colours, here too.
  • Brittany Chiang: a quiet date column beside each role.

Where it started

Before any of this, Husam kept a long list of personal sites he liked, from the loud ones to the plain ones. Several above came straight from it. A few others on it: rauno.me, joshwcomeau.com, lynnandtonic.com, frankchimero.com and maggieappleton.com.
