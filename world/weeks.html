Every Week Since 2015

September 2026 By Husam Sokar
Note: I’m Husam Sokar. Right now I’m co-founder and CEO of a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash’s early team, and a sophomore at Harvard. This page counts how much I’ve had going on at once, one week at a time. Hover over or tap any week to see what was in it. Every role and date is also in a table at the bottom, if you’d rather read than scroll.

The busiest stretch so far: 7 things at once, every week from October 1 to December 31, 2025.

Each row is a year: 2015 at the top, 2026 at the bottom. Each row has 52 boxes, one per week. (The last box in a row also takes the leftover day or two, because years don’t divide evenly into weeks. Nobody has fixed that yet.)

The darker the box, the more things I had going on that week. The red box is this week. The white ones after it are empty because they haven’t happened yet.

611 boxes down. 13 left in 2026.

2015. For the first few years the chart is mostly white. The only thing on it is the orange dot in the corner of each box: most Sundays from 2015 through 2021, I volunteered at the Central California Food Bank.

The record says most Sundays, so read the dots as a habit, not an attendance sheet.

2018. Then Saturdays filled up too. From 2018 through 2021 I volunteered at Masjid Fresno Saturday School most Saturdays. That’s the violet dot.

2020. In March 2020, at 14, I started Electric Deals: retail arbitrage stores on Facebook, plus drop-shipping on eBay. It ran until October. That’s the 35 pale blue boxes.

Facebook: 131+ positive reviews, 400+ products shipped, about $20,000 in revenue at a ~43% margin. eBay: 150+ positive reviews, about $8,000 at ~39%.

2021–2023. In May 2021 I became Lead Advocate at CAIR in Sacramento. It lasted 109 weeks, until May 2023.

I was the lead community advocate for AB 1766 (California IDs for All), working with Asm. Jim Patterson’s office (Legislative Director Ian Coolbear) and Sen. Shannon Grove’s office. I also advocated for AB 1947 (Freedom from Hate Crimes), SB 1161 (Transit Safety), AB 2549 (Ending Street Harassment) and SB 1038 (limits on biometric surveillance by law enforcement).

Five California bills, worked end to end. Meanwhile, in September 2021, I joined the board of the Muslim Community Association of Fresno. That one stays on the chart for 225 weeks.

2022. This is where the boxes get dark. In one year I picked up 8 new titles:

  • April: Executive Director, LegislativeLift
  • May: VP & Executive Director, Muslim Community Association of Fresno
  • June: Congressional Intern, U.S. House
  • July: Senior English Writer, White House in Arabic
  • July: economics research, Chapman University
  • October: economics research, University of Nevada, Reno
  • Sometime that year: Advocacy Fellow, Encode Justice, and Director of Community Relations for the Clovis Community College MSA

By October that added up to 7 things at once, for 4 weeks. The chart wouldn’t reach 7 again for three years.

2024. A year of being one of a small number:

  • 1 of 12 (from 90+ applicants): Research Fellow at Ballotpedia, January to March.
  • 1 of 23: NSDA Student Leadership Council, representing 140,000+ members to the national board, from May.
  • 1 of 5: executives running Diversify Our Narrative, a national 501(c)(3) with 5,000+ student organizers and 800+ district chapters, from June.
  • 1 of 72 (from 2,400+ applicants): the Telluride Association Summer Seminar in Critical Black Studies, Washington DC–Baltimore. I was elected house chair.
  • 1 of 50: paid Election Fellows at Voters of Tomorrow, September and October.

On October 11, The Nation ran “AI Is Taking Over College Admissions” with me as a contributor. The year peaked at 6 things at once.

2025. On April 16 the Wall Street Journal ran “Harvard-Bound Students Stand Firm While Some Columbia Prospects Think Twice,” with me as a contributor. That fall I started at Harvard, concentrating in Applied Math in Government and Economics. Then this happened.

That’s all 624 boxes. Hover over or tap any week to see what’s in it. Switch to Lanes to see every role as its own line.

Or tick Count year-only roles to spread the 10 roles I can only date to a year across their whole years. The count jumps to 13 in early 2023, which tells you more about the method than about 2023.

How I counted

  • Weeks. Each year is 52 weeks starting January 1. Week 52 runs to December 31, so it’s 8 days long (9 in leap years). A role counts in a week if the week’s middle day falls inside the role’s dates.
  • Months. Most dates in my record are months. “Jul–Oct 2022” counts from July 1 to October 31. Two roles that hand off in the same month both count for that month.
  • Seasons, placed approximately. “Summer” means June to August. The two summer programs with a known length (TASS and SPARK, seven weeks) are placed from July 1. The month-long winter fellowship is placed in January 2026.
  • Year-only roles are not counted. 10 roles are dated only to a year (“2023”, “2023–2024”). Spreading each one over its whole year would invent weeks, so by default they don’t count. They still show in the week cards and as hatched bars in Lanes. The checkbox counts them anyway.
  • Weekly volunteering is dots, not count. The food bank (most Sundays, 2015–2021) and Saturday school (most Saturdays, 2018–2021) are drawn in every week of those years. “Most” means some weeks are missing, and I can’t say which.
  • Undated roles are off the chart. Three Harvard roles and the billing and back-office work I did at my father’s law firm have no dates, so they’re in the table but not on the chart. School isn’t counted either.
  • Now. Roles that are still going run to the current week, taken from your device’s clock. The same code computes every number on this page.
