Sources

Sources

How precisely each thing in the record is dated, and what the charts do with it. Each dot is one thing.

Every year, by district: the number of weeks each district had something going on (a week with two things in the same district counts once). Weekend is the standing Saturday and Sunday commitments.

How the counting works. Each year is 52 weeks starting January 1; the last week takes the leftover day or two. A thing counts in a week if the week’s middle day falls inside its dates. Month dates run from the first to the last of the month. Summer means June to August; the two seven-week summer stints are placed from July 1, and the month-long winter one in January 2026. Things dated only to a year are left out of the counts, because spreading them over a whole year would invent weeks. The weekend rows mean most Sundays (2015–2021) and most Saturdays (2018–2021), so read them as a habit, not an attendance sheet. Things still going run to this week, by your device’s date.

The busiest stretch counts only things dated to the month. With the placed winter stint included, the run of 7 continues to January 28, 2026.
