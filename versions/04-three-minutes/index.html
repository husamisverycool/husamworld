Thank you, Presiding Officer.

Representatives, the resolution in front of you asks one thing: affirm Husam Sokar./ Here is my thesis. I learn how institutions actually run, and then I do the work inside them./ Three points: organizing, institutions, and building.

Point one · Organizing

First, organizing. I grew up in Fresno and Clovis, in the Central Valley, doing billing and back-office work at my father’s law firm./ From 2021 to 2025 I helped lead the Muslim Community Association of Fresno, a youth-driven nonprofit, and finished as Vice President and Executive Director. We ran more than 150 community events. We raised more than $240,000 for Syrian and Afghan refugees and Pakistan flood victims./ And we secured a $500,000 pledge for Fresno’s first transitional home for Muslim women, facing homelessness and domestic abuse.slow down

At the same time, in Sacramento, I was CAIR’s lead community advocate for AB 1766, California IDs for All, working with the offices of Assemblymember Jim Patterson and Senator Shannon Grove. I also pushed bills on hate crimes, transit safety, street harassment, and biometric surveillance./ Five California bills, worked end to end.

Point two · Institutions

Second, institutions. At Harvard I don’t just join things; I help run them. I’m a Chair on the senior staff of Harvard Model Congress, directing the Boston conference: North America’s largest congressional simulation, with more than 1,500 delegates from more than 100 schools./ The debater now runs the debate.look up

At the Institute of Politics, I was an analyst on the Harvard Youth Poll, America’s largest poll of 18-to-29-year-olds, and an associate to Ambassador Katherine Tai, the 19th U.S. Trade Representative./ On The Harvard Crimson’s Business Board, I lead international writing and leadership camps. I traveled to South Korea to mentor students and teach two weeks of workshops.

Point three · Building

Third, building. I started at fourteen, with a store called Electric Deals: retail arbitrage on Facebook and drop-shipping on eBay. More than 400 products shipped, and about $20,000 in revenue on Facebook alone, at roughly a 43 percent margin./ Today I’m Co-founder and CEO of a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash’s early team. I spent the summer of 2026 building it full-time in San Francisco. It’s in stealth, so that is all I’ll say from this podium.smile

Impacts

The impact: each point is the same move at a different scale./ In Sacramento, I learned how a bill moves, then worked five of them end to end. At Harvard Model Congress, I learned how a chamber runs, then helped direct one for more than 1,500 delegates./ That is the pattern you’re voting on: learn the institution, then do the work inside it.

Conclusion

Representatives, I’m a sophomore from the Central Valley studying Applied Math in Government and Economics. I’ve organized a community, worked inside the institutions that write the rules, and started building my own./ For these reasons, I proudly urge an affirmation, and I’m happy to yield to questions.

    Questioner 1What did you actually do at CAIR?

    A.From May 2021 to May 2023 I was a Lead Advocate for CAIR in Sacramento. I was the lead community advocate for AB 1766, California IDs for All, working with Assemblymember Jim Patterson’s office (his Legislative Director, Ian Coolbear) and Senator Shannon Grove’s office. I also advocated for:

    • AB 1947, Freedom from Hate Crimes
    • SB 1161, Transit Safety
    • AB 2549, Ending Street Harassment
    • SB 1038, limits on biometric surveillance by law enforcement

    Five California bills, worked end to end.

    Questioner 2What’s the startup?

    A.I’m Co-founder & CEO of a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash’s early team. I’ve been on it full-time since June 2026 and spent the summer building it in San Francisco. It’s in stealth, so that’s the full answer for now.

    Questioner 3Why Arabic?

    A.It’s one of my two working languages. I hold the California State Seal of Biliteracy in Arabic, and I translate professionally between Arabic and English.

    From July 2022 to April 2024 I was Senior English Writer at White House in Arabic, editing and translating press briefings, op-eds, and documentary scripts under Marah Bukai, Washington Bureau Chief of Al-Arab Media Organization. That work reached 350,000+ combined readers and viewers. A few pieces I edited or translated:

    • MalikiLeaks: When Sectarianism Rules Iraq
    • The Sadrist Movement’s Sit-In Sends Iraq into Political Turmoil
    • The Possible Outcomes of the Iraqi Political Crisis
    • Biden in Palestine: Is Health a Path to Statehood?
    • Iraq to Play a Major Role at Jeddah Summit
    • Mustafa Al-Kazemi’s Crucial Role in the MENA Region
    Questioner 4What have you won?

    A.In this event: NSDA National Tournament Finalist in Congressional Debate, 1 of 20 from 50,000+. Outside it: California Mock Trial Finals MVP, NSDA Student of the Year, Coca-Cola Scholar, Gates Scholar, and the Princeton Prize in Race Relations. The full record is on the ballot below.
