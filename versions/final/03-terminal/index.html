    Husam Sokar

    Co-founder & Chief Executive Officer · since Jun 2026

    Grew up in Fresno and Clovis, in California's Central Valley. At 14 he ran two online stores: a Facebook retail-arbitrage shop (~$20K revenue, ~43% margin) and an eBay drop-shipping store (~$8K, ~39%). He also did billing and back-office work as an admin assistant at his father's law firm.

    Then the operator years. As VP & Executive Director of the Muslim Community Association of Fresno, a youth-driven nonprofit, he ran 150+ events, raised $240K+ for Syrian and Afghan refugees and Pakistan flood victims, and secured a $500K pledge for Fresno's first Muslim women's transitional home. As CAIR's lead community advocate in Sacramento, he worked AB 1766 (California IDs for All) and four more California bills.

    At Harvard (Applied Math in Government and Economics) he has worked inside the institutions: analyst on the Harvard Youth Poll, associate to Ambassador Katherine Tai at the Institute of Politics, project lead on The Harvard Crimson's business side (including two weeks of workshops in South Korea), and chair of Harvard Model Congress, North America's largest congressional simulation.

    Also: Arabic↔English translator for White House in Arabic (350K+ readers and viewers), NSDA National Finalist in Congressional Debate, California Mock Trial Finals MVP.

    In June 2026 he co-founded a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash's early team, and spent the summer building it full-time in San Francisco.
