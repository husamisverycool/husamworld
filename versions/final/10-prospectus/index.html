      Business

      Overview

      The Founder’s business, to date, has been learning how an institution actually runs and then doing the work inside it. He has run real operations, with real money and real people, since he was a teenager. The Company is the first time he is building the institution himself.

      The Community Organization

      From May 2022 to December 2025 the Founder was VP & Executive Director of the Community Organization, after serving from September 2021 as a board member and in outreach and relations roles. Under his leadership it ran 150+ community events, raised $240K+ for Syrian and Afghan refugees and Pakistan flood victims, and secured a $500K pledge for Fresno’s first Muslim women’s transitional home, addressing homelessness and domestic abuse.5 It also ran clothing and food drives and mentoring groups at local mosques and Islamic schools.

      Legislative operations

      From 2021 to 2023, with CAIR in Sacramento, the Founder was lead community advocate for AB 1766 (California IDs for All). He worked with Assemblymember Jim Patterson’s office and Senator Shannon Grove’s office, and advocated for AB 1947, SB 1161, AB 2549 and SB 1038: the Bills, five in total, worked end to end.

      Harvard institutions

      • Harvard Model Congress. Chair (Senior Staff) since January 2026, on staff since September 2025. Directing the Boston conference of the Simulation (1,500+ delegates, 100+ schools); also staffing HMC Europe (500+ delegates, 20+ countries).
      • The Harvard Crimson Business Board. Project Team Lead for TPR/AlgoEd since December 2025, leading international writing and leadership camps for the Newspaper with The Princeton Review and AlgoEd. Earlier, as Global Programs Associate, he judged international rounds of the Harvard Crimson Global Quiz Bowl and traveled to South Korea to lead two weeks of workshops.
      • Institute of Politics, Harvard Kennedy School. Analyst on the Harvard Youth Poll, America’s largest poll of 18–29-year-olds; associate to Ambassador Katherine Tai, 19th U.S. Trade Representative.
      • Harvard Global Research and Consulting Group. Senior Associate, consulting for UNICEF and the Urban Institute.

      Earlier operations

      At 14 the Founder ran the Stores. He did billing and back-office work as an admin assistant at his father’s law firm. He translated between Arabic and English for White House in Arabic from 2022 to 2024, and did econometrics and economics research at the University of Nevada, Reno, the University of Houston and Chapman University in 2022 and 2023.

      He has contributed to reporting in the Wall Street Journal and The Nation. See “Where You Can Find More Information.”

      He has run a nonprofit, pushed bills through Sacramento, and helped run some of Harvard’s biggest student institutions. Before that, two online stores at 14.
