§ 1

Key findings

  1. The respondent's pattern holds across every wave: he learns how an institution actually runs, then does the work inside it. Observed in a Sacramento bill campaign, a congressional office, a translation desk, a debate league, a youth poll, and now a company.
  2. He is currently Co-founder & CEO of a stealth startup, backed by DoorDash's early team, which he spent summer 2026 building full-time in San Francisco.
  3. Between 2021 and 2025, a youth-run Fresno nonprofit under his leadership held 150+ community events, raised $240K+ for refugees and flood victims, and secured a $500K pledge for Fresno's first Muslim women's transitional home.
  4. Where selection rates were published, the median honor or program in this report took 1 in 33.2 The most selective honor took at most 1 in 2,500 (Figure 1).

Appendix A

Methodology

Population
Husam Sokar.
Sample
The entire population (n = 1). No sampling error is possible; measurement error is the respondent's responsibility.
Frame
Fresno and Clovis, California (2015–2025); Sacramento; Reno, NV; Orange, CA; Washington, DC–Baltimore; Cambridge and Boston, MA (2025–); San Francisco (summer 2026); South Korea; Lithuania, Poland, and Hungary; Puerto Rico; Europe.
Mode
Self-administered, in English. Arabic available on request.
Weighting
None. The sample already matches the population on every demographic.
Instrument
Questions were written to be answered with counts from the respondent's records. Opinion items were dropped at the pretest stage.
Background covariates
Grew up doing billing and back-office work as an admin assistant at his father's law firm. Summa Cum Laude, Clovis Community College. Concentrating in Applied Math in Government and Economics.
Disclosure
The analyst and the respondent are the same person. Readers should weigh the findings accordingly.
